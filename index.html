1. Automated Applicant Screening & AI Rating System

An automated n8n recruitment workflow that captures job application form submissions and creates a preliminary entry in Airtable. A Switch node filters candidates by applied positions (Software Engineer, AI Engineer, Video Editor, Coder). The applicant data is passed to an AI Agent powered by Google Gemini, which evaluates skill demand, assigns a star rating (5-star to 2-star), and automatically updates the candidate's database record with evaluation metrics.
